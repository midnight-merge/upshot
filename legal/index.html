Privacy and terms

Privacy

Everything after the # stays in your browser. Browsers never send it to a server, so this site receives no part of a card, and everything the page does, it does on your own device. Numbers you type into one are no exception: they are worked out there and written back into your own link, and never sent anywhere. Nothing to store, nothing to delete.

GitHub Pages logs IPs to serve the page - a legitimate interest, and the only personal data involved. GitHub holds those logs; Midnight Merge holds nothing. Nothing is kept on your device and nothing is fetched from anywhere: no cookies, no third-party scripts, no fonts, no analytics.

The card rides inside the link, so anyone holding the link can read it, as can your chat history. Private from us is not private in general.

Terms

Cards are written by whichever AI you asked. They can be wrong, and a formula is no safer than a sentence - a card shows its working, which proves the sum and not the reasoning behind it. Check anything that matters.

Anyone can put any text in a link, so text on this domain is not published or endorsed by Midnight Merge, and never reaches us to moderate or remove.
