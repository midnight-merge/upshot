# One ask per line, read by scripts/gen-corpus.js and sent to the model as
# "export this\n\n<line>". Blank lines and # lines are ignored. The label in
# the run output and the corpus comments is the text before the dash.
#
# Each is a calculation whose arithmetic is easy once you know the model, and
# whose model is the domain knowledge. The factors after the dash are what the
# card has to carry as inputs.

# Around the house
Solar panel yield — panel size, orientation, latitude, season, weather loss, inverter efficiency.
Heating/cooling requirement — room volume, insulation, temperature delta, windows, occupancy.
Mortgage overpayment impact — rate, term, remaining balance, timing of overpayments.
EV charging time — battery %, pack size, charger power, charging curve, losses.
Paint needed for a room — geometry, doors/windows, coats, paint coverage, waste.
Concrete/material quantities — shape dimensions, density, wastage, mix ratios.
Rainwater collection — roof area, rainfall, runoff coefficient, tank size.
Watering requirement for plants — pot size, plant type, temperature, humidity, soil.

# Science
Radioactive decay / remaining isotope — half-life plus elapsed time.
Drug concentration over time — dose, half-life, dosing interval, accumulation.
Projectile trajectory — velocity, angle, height, gravity, drag approximation.
Sound level at distance — source dB, distance, multiple sources, environment.
Telescope resolving power — aperture, wavelength, atmosphere.
Camera depth of field — focal length, aperture, distance, sensor format.
Exposure equivalence — shutter, aperture, ISO, ND filter.
Dew point / condensation risk — temperature and relative humidity.

# Human body
Blood alcohol estimate over time — drinks, strength, timing, body characteristics.
Calories burned hiking — weight, terrain, elevation, speed, load carried.
Hydration requirement — body mass, activity, heat, duration, sweat rate.
Sleep debt/recovery estimate — usual need, recent sleep, naps.
1RM estimate — weight/reps using different models.

# Money and business
Freelance day rate needed — desired salary, holidays, utilisation, expenses, tax overhead.
SaaS unit economics — CAC, churn, margin, ARPU, expansion.
Inventory reorder point — sales velocity, lead time, variability, safety stock.
Employee true cost — salary, pension, NI, equipment, office, benefits.
Rent vs buy — appreciation, mortgage, rent growth, maintenance, opportunity cost.
Subscription true annual cost — tiers, usage limits, overages, discounts.
Currency conversion after real costs — FX rate, spread, fees, card charges.

# Travel and logistics
Road-trip arrival time — route, speed profiles, stops, charging/refuelling.
Flight jet-lag direction/severity — time zones crossed, direction, departure/arrival.
Luggage weight distribution — item weights, bag limits, combinations.
Walking time through terrain — distance, elevation gain, surface.

# Music, audio and art
Delay/reverb timing from BPM — dotted/triplet subdivisions.
Pitch transposition — semitones, cents, frequency.
String tension — scale length, tuning, string gauge.
Print size from an image — pixels, DPI, crop ratio.
Colour mixing proportions — target colour and available pigments, approximately.

# Everyday weird but useful
How much food for a party — guests, adults/kids, meal type, duration, leftovers.
Coffee brew recipe scaler — beans, ratio, extraction style, target output.
Freezer cooling time — mass, starting temp, material, freezer power approximation.
How long until a room reaches temperature — heater power plus thermal losses.
Moving-box estimator — rooms, household size, item categories.
Wi-Fi/link budget — transmit power, antenna gain, frequency, distance, obstacles.
Password brute-force time — character set, length, guesses/sec.
Queue/wait-time estimate — arrival rate, service rate, number of servers.

# Random
Speeding car and time saved — speed, speed limit, distance.
