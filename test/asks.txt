# The fixed ask-set.
#
# Fifteen asks, in plain conversational English, crossed with every model you
# can reach. THE LIST DOES NOT CHANGE. That is the whole point of it: a fixed
# set is what makes next month's run comparable to this month's. Add to the
# bottom if something important is missing, never edit or reword what is here.
#
# HOW TO RUN IT
#
#   1. A fresh chat per ask, per model. No history, no earlier cards to copy.
#   2. Either paste the whole of llms.txt verbatim first - nothing added, no
#      "please follow this format" framing - or, once it is deployed, say
#      "read https://upshot.fyi and export this". Note which you did; a pasted
#      spec arrives as your instruction and gets followed more obediently, so
#      it gives the optimistic number.
#   3. Then the ask, exactly as written below.
#   4. Paste every link that comes back into a file, one per line. Including
#      the broken ones - especially those. Do not tidy them, do not retry, do
#      not fix them by hand. What came back is the data.
#   5. node test/transport.js links.txt
#   6. Then READ THE NUMBERS ON THE CARDS. A card can pass every structural
#      check and still print a wrong answer, and that is where the two worst
#      bugs in this format have both been found.
#
# WHAT YOU ARE MEASURING
#
# Two counts, and the second matters more.
#
#   valid first render   - did a model get the spec right
#   invented syntax      - did a model reach for something the grammar has no
#                          way to say, and make it up
#
# The second is the grammar coming up short rather than the model. One
# invention turning up again and again is a feature request. Many different
# ones mean the vocabulary is too weak.
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

# a calculator, the commonest shape there is
1  Four of us shared a taxi and it was 38 quid, work out what we each owe

# a result feeding another result
2  I want to know what my takeaway coffee habit costs me a year if it is
   3.40 a cup and I buy one on weekdays

# how long until, which is what ln is for
3  I owe 4200 on a card at 24 percent and pay 120 a month, how long till it
   is gone

# what something grows to, which is what exp is for
4  If I leave 14 grand in an index fund at about 6 and a half percent and
   never touch it, what is it worth in eleven years

# a how-to, where order is the point and there are no numbers
5  How do I bleed a radiator without soaking the carpet

# a comparison, which is two labelled blocks
6  Train or drive from London to Leeds, which is better

# a spec sheet, with values that are not numbers
7  Summarise the specs of the laptop we just talked about

# a checklist the reader works through
8  Give me a checklist for getting my rental deposit back

# a checklist that scores itself and makes a call
9  Help me work out if I actually need income protection insurance

# pick-one, where the option carries the number
10 What do I repay on my student loan, I am not sure which plan I am on

# a three-way threshold, which is what the cascade is for
11 Tell me how worried to be about a toddler crying based on how long it
   has been going on

# a range, which needs a chained comparison
12 Can I get the cheaper railcard, I am 30

# PRESSURE ON THE KNOWN WALL - a banded NUMBER, not banded wording. The
# language cannot do this. Watch what a model does instead: whether it fakes
# it with a cascade, invents a conditional, or hard-codes one band and drops
# the rest. This is the most informative ask in the list.
13 Work out my income tax on 62 grand, with the proper bands

14 What stamp duty do I pay on a 420k house as a first time buyer

# a duration answer, the old 5.64-not-5:38 soft spot
15 How long does the drive take if it is 290 miles at about 62 mph with two
   twenty minute stops
