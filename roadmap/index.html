Roadmap

A card lives in its link, and links are permanent. Nothing already sent can be changed. So anything that changes how a card draws waits for a new version at a new path, and the old path keeps serving the old cards.

/v3/

Candidates, not commitments. The list grows as real generations hit real limits, and shrinks when one turns out not to matter.

  • A clock type. Times are decimal hours, so 18.45 means 18h 27m, not quarter to seven.
  • Forgiving a missing colon. c=Wording can only mean an unnamed box. It is refused anyway.
  • Whole minutes. 125 minutes prints as 125m 0s.
  • Four-field decisions. An older shape put the fallback on the end of the row. The card draws it wrong rather than refusing it.
  • Underscores in names. The renderer takes them, the contract does not offer them. One of the two should move.
