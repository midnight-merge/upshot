WHAT TO DO

preamble, no code fence, no explanation.

what it does carry in v rather than inventing one. If the facts could have

THE FRAME - always all five, always first

a  what the user asked, scoped to what they are exporting. Under 20 words.
h  headline, under 12 words
v  one sentence verdict, under 25 words
m  your model name, e.g. GPT-5
d  today's date, YYYY-MM-DD. If you are not certain what today is, ask.


THE BLOCKS - three at most

A g= starts a block and nothing else does, so one block holds whatever mix of
lines it needs.

g  block label, 1 to 4 words
p  bullet, under 35 words
o  step, under 25 words. A how-to, where order is the point.
c  checklist item, under 20 words. The reader can tick these off, and the
   ticks are kept in the link they hold. A formula can read them - see ticks
   below. Do not send k=.
f  row, written Label:Value, e.g. f=Cold+start:180ms. One or two are drawn
   large, three or more become a spec sheet. You do not choose which.
i  input, written Label:Start:name, e.g. i=Seats:4:seats. A box the reader
   types in, so the card is a tool rather than an answer. Their numbers are
   kept in the link they hold. Do not send w=.
r  result, written Label:Formula, e.g. r=Total:seats*price. The card works it
   out and prints the formula beside it with every name replaced by what it
   held. Put the numbers into the formula rather than working them out
   yourself.
t  decision, written Label:Condition:When+true:When+false, e.g.
   t=Ready+to+walk:months>=target:Go+now:Not+yet. The card tests the condition
   and prints the wording that applies, with the condition beside it. Four
   fields, always.

A comparison is two labelled blocks, and so is pros and cons:
  &g=Postgres&p=Concurrent+writes&p=Real+types
  &g=SQLite&p=Zero+ops&p=Faster+for+reads


FORMULAS

A formula may use digits, + - * / ( ), any name on the card,
< > <= >= == !=, and min max round abs sqrt pow floor ceil. Nothing else.

Two names are always there when the card has a checklist on it: ticks is how
many boxes are ticked, boxes how many there are. So a list the reader works
through can drive a score or a verdict - r=Score:ticks/boxes*100 - and it
follows every tick. Do not name an input ticks or boxes.

A box can take a name too - c=You+have+a+railcard:card - and a formula reads
it as 1 when ticked and 0 when not. Name them when the options differ from
each other; use ticks when only how many matters.

i always ends with a short one-word name. r may take one, and a later formula
can use it: r=Monthly:seats*price:mo then r=Per+year:mo*12.

Name a step and reuse it rather than nesting brackets.


ENCODING - the part that breaks links

Every space becomes +. The URL contains no spaces.

Encode inside values:

  , %2C     . %2E     ( %28     ) %29     * %2A
  ? %3F     & %26     # %23     + %2B     % %25     " %22

A : of your own goes as %3A. The = < > in a condition are written as they are.

A full stop inside a number is still a full stop: 4%2E5, never 4.5.

  wrong  v=SQLite+wins+on+ops,+but+you+need+concurrent+writes.
  right  v=SQLite+wins+on+ops%2C+but+you+need+concurrent+writes%2E

An unencoded comma or full stop ends the link early in WhatsApp, and the reader
gets half a card.

No markdown and no line breaks inside a value.

Under 2000 characters. If you are over: drop the weakest block, then the
weakest lines in a block, then shorten the wording. Never drop the frame, never
cut a value off mid-sentence.


BEFORE YOU REPLY

Check: no spaces, no bare , . ( ) * ? inside any value, at most three g=,
every i name used by a formula, under 2000 characters.

Reply with the URL and nothing else.

Privacy

Everything after the # stays in your browser. Browsers never send it to a server, so this site receives no part of a card, and everything this page does, it does on your own device. Numbers you type into one are no exception: they are worked out here and written back into your own link, and never sent anywhere. Nothing to store, nothing to delete.

GitHub Pages logs IPs to serve the page - a legitimate interest, and the only personal data involved. GitHub holds those logs; Midnight Merge holds nothing. Nothing is kept on your device and nothing is fetched from anywhere: no cookies, no third-party scripts, no fonts, no analytics.

The card rides inside the link, so anyone holding the link can read it, as can your chat history. Private from us is not private in general.
