upshot.fyi

Turn a chat into a link you can send

Tell your AI: read upshot.fyi and export this

No account, nothing to install. The whole export lives in the link.

Format

For any AI reading this page.

https://upshot.fyi/#s=explainer&m=MODEL&d=YYYY-MM-DD&a=ASK&h=HEADLINE&v=VERDICT&p=POINT&p=POINT

s  shape. Only "explainer" exists right now.
m  your model name, e.g. GPT-5
d  today's date, YYYY-MM-DD
a  one line on what the user asked, scoped to what they are exporting, under 20 words
h  headline, under 12 words
v  one sentence verdict, under 25 words
p  one key point, under 35 words. Use 2 to 4. Repeat p= for each.

RULES
Every space becomes + . The URL must contain no spaces.
Encode inside values:  , %2C   . %2E   & %26   = %3D   # %23   + %2B   % %25   " %22
Commas and full stops MUST be encoded or WhatsApp breaks the link.
Plain sentences only. No markdown, no line breaks inside values.
Keep it readable on one phone screen. The word limits above do this.
If facts could have changed recently, search first. Do not write from memory.

Reply with the URL and nothing else.

Privacy

Everything after the # stays in your browser. Browsers never send it to a server, so this site receives no part of an export. Nothing to store, nothing to delete.

GitHub Pages logs IPs to serve the page. Nothing else is fetched from anywhere - no third-party scripts, no fonts, no cookies, no analytics. Share image is drawn in your browser and never uploaded.

The export rides inside the link, so anyone holding the link can read it, as can your chat history. Private from us is not private in general.

Terms

Exports are written by whichever AI you asked. They can be wrong. Check anything that matters.

Anyone can put any text in a link, so text on this domain is not published or endorsed by Midnight Merge, and never reaches us to moderate or remove.
